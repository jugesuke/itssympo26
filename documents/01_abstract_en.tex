We are building a GTFS-based bus signage system that installers at small bus stops can configure themselves.
However, GTFS lacks the semantic information that passenger displays need, such as the internal structure of headsigns and the ``direction'' seen from a stop.
We propose a method that combines an AI agent with GTFS analysis tools to generate this information automatically.
The agent extracts destinations, via points, and service types from headsigns, correcting any that fail a check against actual stop names.
In an evaluation with GTFS data from three operators, all headsigns passed the check, and the directions largely matched the operators' classification.
